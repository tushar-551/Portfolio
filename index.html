I'm a Power BI Developer and Data Analyst based in Pune, with two years of hands-on work across finance and healthcare.

My most concrete result: I built a SQL-based risk scoring model for a loan recovery team that was consistently reacting to defaults too late. The model weighted overdue history, missed payments, and customer profile data to flag high-risk accounts before they defaulted. Defaults dropped 18% and collections recovered $1.1M+. I also built the Power BI reporting layer and automated the weekly alerts that gave the team time to act.

In healthcare, I set up a live patient vitals pipeline from Cassandra into Power BI, with threshold-based alert logic so hospital staff could respond to critical events in real time, not after the fact.

I recently cleared the DP-600 exam (Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate), which reflects where I'm heading with cloud-scale analytics and Microsoft Fabric. My core tools are Power BI, DAX, SQL, and Python. What I care about is whether the output actually changes how a team makes decisions. If it doesn't, the dashboard isn't done.
